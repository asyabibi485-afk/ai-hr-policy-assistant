COMPANY HR POLICY

1. WORKING HOURS

Normal working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM,
Monday to Friday.

2. ANNUAL LEAVE

Employees are entitled to annual leave according
to company policy and their employment contract.

3. SICK LEAVE

Employees who are unable to attend work because of
illness should notify their manager as soon as possible.

4. ATTENDANCE

Employees are expected to arrive on time and maintain
regular attendance.

5. REMOTE WORK

Remote work requires prior approval from the employee's
manager.

6. DISCIPLINARY ACTION

Violation of company policies may result in disciplinary
action according to company procedures.

7. HARASSMENT

Harassment, discrimination and inappropriate workplace
behavior are prohibited.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY

Employees must protect confidential company and employee
information.
